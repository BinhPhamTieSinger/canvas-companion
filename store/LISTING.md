# Canvas Companion

## Short description
An integrated VinUni Canvas timetable, source-update review queue and personal background workspace.

## Detailed description
Organize your academic week and personalize VinUniversity Canvas from one integrated workspace.

• Open the Companion panel from Canvas's sidebar for a workspace overview and appearance settings.
• View classes, recitations, labs, examinations, assignment deadlines, projects and milestones in daily, weekly, monthly and combined views.
• Match your Canvas courses against a bundled Fall 2026 catalog of 389 sections across 222 course codes. Confirm your registered section before importing. Portal login is not required; 10 sections have no published times.
• Refresh structured Canvas planner and calendar information without creating duplicate assignments. Optional refresh runs every 15 minutes while the dashboard is open.
• Review new or edited Canvas Home pages, native syllabus bodies and recent announcements. A red review banner, keyword topic hints, previous excerpts and source links help you decide what to update. Manual academic records change only when you save an edit; dates and rooms are not invented.
• Keep one background configuration with image upload or HTTPS URL, positioning, cover/contain, scaling, brightness, blur, overlay and local backups. Transparent surfaces use readable glass panels and light text.
• See informational warnings for overlapping events, short travel gaps, same-day examinations and concentrated deadlines.
• Store personal records and image uploads locally in your Chrome profile. No publisher server, analytics, advertisements or credential collection.

IMPORTANT: Live integration is currently limited to https://vinuni.instructure.com. This is a dated Fall 2026 catalog, not a live registration service. Confirm your section, session types, rooms and later changes. Freeform academic prose needs human review. External Simple Syllabus embeds and arbitrary attachments are not automatically monitored.

Try the sample workspace from the extension popup without a university account. It contains fictional data and resets on reload.

Canvas Companion is independent of Instructure, VinUniversity and BetterCampus. It does not change Canvas submissions, grades, registration or messages. Background and manual academic data do not synchronize across devices. Export backups before uninstalling or moving Chrome profiles.

## Reviewer instructions
Install the extension and open its popup. Choose “Try the sample workspace” to explore the actual timetable, review banner, source review and manual editors with fictional in-memory data and no Canvas login. Sample changes reset on reload. The separate Appearance settings page can be tested without university credentials.

Live integration requires a student-authorized VinUni Canvas session. No test university account is provided and no credentials should be requested from students. Visit the university's official login page and sign in only if your own authorized account is available. On the dashboard, use the sidebar “Companion” button or the Academic timetable. New and changed source documents are review prompts rather than automatic prose-to-calendar updates.

## Purpose and permissions
Single purpose: a local academic planning and appearance workspace integrated into VinUni Canvas.
Storage: persist background configuration/image assets, account-scoped manual academic records and source caches, and notify other open extension views about revisions.
Host access: render the workspace on https://vinuni.instructure.com and read authorized courses, planner/calendar data, native syllabus/Home pages and announcements using GET requests. No other LMS or registration portal host access is requested.
Remote code: none. All executable code is packaged with the extension. Image URLs load images only; Canvas APIs return data only.
